# PRAANJEEV ❤️

## AI Emergency Response & Hospital Coordination System

> **“In the race against time, we choose life.”**

🌐 **Live Demo:** https://praanjeevai.vercel.app/

PRAANJEEV is an AI-powered emergency response and hospital coordination system designed to help patients, families, ambulance teams, and hospitals coordinate critical emergency care more efficiently.

### 💡 Core Idea

**The nearest hospital is not always the right hospital.**

PRAANJEEV considers emergency condition, hospital capabilities, resource availability, verification, traffic, and ETA to support better emergency coordination.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

During an emergency, important information is often fragmented:

- Family searches for hospitals
- Hospital availability is unclear
- ICU and emergency resources may not be known
- Ambulance needs a suitable destination
- Traffic can change the ETA
- Hospitals may receive limited information before arrival
- Emergency preparation may start late

### Our Goal

> **Right Hospital • Right Information • Right Time**
